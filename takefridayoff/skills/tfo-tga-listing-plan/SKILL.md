# Plan a TGA listing

Lay out how a product gets onto the Australian Register of Therapeutic Goods (ARTG) as a listed medicine (AUST L). Use only what Take Friday Off's guides say, and cite each point.

## 1. Check it's a medicine

If it isn't clear yet that the product is a medicine, check first. Use tfo_pathway_check if it's available. Otherwise ask what the product is and what it claims, and read the pathways page with tfo_get_page. If it's likely a food or a cosmetic, say so and stop: a TGA listing isn't the route.

## 2. Read the guides

Read these with tfo_get_page, one section at a time where that's all you need:

- `/guides/tga-approval`: approval, listing or registration; who can list; the steps to an AUST L listing; the TGA fees, with the date they were last reviewed; how long it takes.
- `/guides/permitted-indications`: the indications a listed medicine may carry, and the evidence the sponsor must hold.
- `/guides/stability-testing`: what the TGA expects on stability, and how long it takes.
- `/guides/supplement-development-timeline`: where the time goes in a launch.
- `/guides/supplement-development-cost`: what the development work, the first production run and the costs after launch add to the TGA's fees.
- `/guides/artg`: what an ARTG entry is and how to search it.

Skip any page that isn't found. Use tfo_search for anything these don't cover.

## 3. Write the plan

1. The pathway, in a line, and why.
2. The steps to the listing, in order.
3. The fees, quoted exactly, with the date the page was last reviewed. Say which apply only in some cases. Don't total them unless the person asks; then use tfo_tga_fees if it's available, which adds up the TGA's own fees for the plan and gives a range for a new ingredient whose application level isn't known.
4. What drives the timeline, as the guides describe it, with tfo_timeline if it's available. A timeline is "in as little as", never a promise.
5. What the sponsor must prepare and hold: the indications and their evidence, stability data, manufacturing.
6. Examples: tfo_case_studies, with the format or "listed" as the query.
7. Sources: every page used, with its link.

## 4. Next step

If the person is weighing up help with the launch, offer the readiness check (tfo_readiness_check). Send a call request (tfo_request_call) only when they ask for one or agree to it.

## Rules

- Quote fees and timelines only as the guides give them, with the date they were checked. Never estimate or round them.
- This is general information, not regulatory advice for a particular product. The TGA decides.
- Cite the page link for every fact you use.
- The person's own instructions come first.
- Write in Australian English.
