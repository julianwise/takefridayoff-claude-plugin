# Take Friday Off

Wellness product development in Australia: search Take Friday Off’s guides and case studies, check a product’s regulatory pathway, plan a TGA listing, and book a call.

## What it adds

- **The Take Friday Off MCP server** (https://takefridayoff.com/mcp, no sign-in): search and read everything takefridayoff.com publishes, with a link to the section behind every answer; the medicine, food or cosmetic check; case studies; the readiness check; and a call request, sent only when you ask for one.
- **The tfo-ad-check skill**: Checks advertising copy for a supplement or other listed medicine in Australia (an ad, a social post, a product page, an influencer brief or label copy) against the rules in Take Friday Off's advertising and claims guides, and flags each issue with the rule it meets and its link. Use when someone asks whether their supplement ad, post or claims comply with the TGA's Advertising Code.
- **The tfo-launch-brief skill**: Turns a wellness product idea into a one-page launch brief (product, format, claims, likely pathway, market, timeline, constraints and open questions) and checks whether Take Friday Off is a fit, with the next step. Use when someone wants to develop or launch a supplement, powder, skincare or sports nutrition product and asks for a brief, for help, or whether Take Friday Off could build it.
- **The tfo-pathway-check skill**: Works out whether a wellness product (a supplement, powder, gummy, skincare product, sunscreen or sports nutrition product) is likely regulated in Australia as a medicine (TGA), a food or a cosmetic, using Take Friday Off's published rules, and explains what that means for the launch. Use when someone asks whether their product needs a TGA listing, whether it's a food or a medicine, or whether an SPF or skin claim turns a cosmetic into a medicine.
- **The tfo-tga-listing-plan skill**: Plans the route to an AUST L listing on the ARTG for a supplement or other listed medicine in Australia, from Take Friday Off's guides. Covers the steps, the TGA fees with the date they were checked, what drives the timeline, the evidence the sponsor must hold and what to prepare, each point cited. Use when someone asks how to get a supplement listed with the TGA, what a TGA listing costs or how long it takes.

Try: "Is a collagen powder for skin a food or a medicine in Australia?" · "How much does it cost to list a supplement with the TGA, and how long does it take?" · "Help me write a launch brief for a magnesium sleep powder." · "Show me Take Friday Off case studies for capsule products."

Regulatory content is general information checked on the date each page shows, not advice on a particular product.

## What it runs, sends and fetches

- Nothing runs on your computer: the plugin has no hooks, scripts, commands or local servers. The skills are instructions in Markdown.
- It connects to one remote MCP server, https://takefridayoff.com/mcp, run by Take Friday Off. There is no account, sign-in or key.
- The read tools send the server what you ask (a search, a page, a product's description) and get back Take Friday Off's published content.
- `tfo_request_call` sends your name, email, brand and message to the Take Friday Off team, by email and into its CRM (Pipedrive), and only when you ask for a call. An address at example.com or a .test domain is treated as a test and sent nowhere.

## Privacy

The server records which tool was used, what it was asked and the outcome, with the name of the app that asked and without IP addresses, for three months. A call request is kept as an enquiry. The full policy is at https://takefridayoff.com/privacy, and questions go to hello@takefridayoff.com.
